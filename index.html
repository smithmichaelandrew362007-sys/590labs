ORIGINS & ETHOS // 590 LABS®

STREETWEAR AS A COUNTER-CULTURE CANVAS.

"We build clothing for people who treat getting dressed as an act of personal autonomy rather than corporate compliance."

590 LABS was conceived in 2024 as an independent antidote to commercialized, homogenized streetwear. In a world saturated with synthetic polyester blends and fast-fashion drops that end up in landfills, we committed to an uncompromising physical standard: every piece must feel substantial, intentional, and indestructible.

We source single-origin combed organic cotton from certified mills in Tirupur, Southern India, knitting our bespoke jerseys at 280 GSM to 450 GSM. We wash our raw fabrics with enzyme and mineral treatments before precision tailoring, eliminating the shrinkage and twisting that plague ordinary T-shirts after the first wash.

Our sizing is intentionally unisex and generous. We believe the silhouette should be dictated by personal styling rather than rigid gender categorizations. Every seam is double-locked or coverstitched to withstand real urban daily wear.
